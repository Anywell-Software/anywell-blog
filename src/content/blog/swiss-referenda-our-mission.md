Well, there is one problem by design. In Switzerland we do have votes about four times a year. During those four times, there are mostly 2 national changes, 2 cantonal and maybe 1 communal change being voted on. So imagine that. 5 proposals times four. This makes 20 proposals. Actually, that is great. This shows how much the Swiss people can affect their country. But still, voter turnout in Switzerland consistently sits at around 45 to 50 percent.
