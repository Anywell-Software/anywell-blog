## Problem explanation

Well, there is one problem by design. In Switzerland we do have votes about four times a year. During those four times, there are mostly 2 national changes, 2 cantonal and maybe 1 communal change being voted on. So imagine that. 5 proposals times four. This makes 20 proposals. Actually, that is great. This shows how much the Swiss people can affect their country. But still, voter turnout in Switzerland consistently sits at around 45 to 50 percent. Actually the last election from the 27th of September only resulted into a turnout of around 47%.

## Reasons

So naturally the question is, why this happens. We tried to find this over the last few weeks and came to two words which show the problem:
It's called: "Infobesity" and "Illiteracy".

### Infobesity

If you ever received a Swiss Election Ballot, you would have seen how many things are in there.  In the worst case scenario the election contains of voting on a Communal, Cantonal and Federal level with each 4 proposals to vote on. With that, you will have 12 proposals which all might be about a different topic to vote on. Some times the proposals might have counter proposals aswell, which would go into additional knowledge to be understood by the people of Switzerland.
